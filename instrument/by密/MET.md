Mechano-electrical transduction
MET is the mechanism by which the mechanical sound signal is converted into electrical signals. It occurs in specialized receptor cells of the inner ear, called hair cells. In mammals, hair cells are located within the organ of Corti.

![[Pasted image 20250503184529.png]]


- Vibration of the basilar membrane leads to deflection of the hair bundles of the hair cells, through the micromechanics of the organ of Corti
- Vibration of hair bundles opens and closes ion channels, changing the cell's membrane potential
- A change in the membrane potential triggers the release of neurotransmitters and action potentials in attached auditory-nerve fibers

- Weak signals are amplified more than loud signals =>compressive nonlinearity in the response to the amplitude of a tone
