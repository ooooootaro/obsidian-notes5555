**Why combine them for a low-pass filter?**

- By strategic placement of resistors and [[capacitors]], you can create a circuit that allows low frequencies to pass through while attenuating high frequencies
- The cutoff frequency is determined by the values of R and C (fc = 1/(2πRC))
- For ECG signals, the important frequencies are typically below 100-150Hz, while noise often exists at higher frequencies
- The RC low-pass filter removes high-frequency noise like muscle artifacts, electromagnetic interference, and power line noise (50/60Hz) while preserving the diagnostic features of the ECG waveform
